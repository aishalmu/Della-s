    Copied ✓

    iPhone or iPad
    1. On your iPhone, tap Add to my iPhone calendar and choose Subscribe.
    2. If nothing happens, copy the link above, then go to Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, paste it and tap Next → Save.
    3. To see new bookings sooner, go to Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Fetch New Data and pick Every 15 minutes.
    Google Calendar
    1. On a computer, open Google Calendar → Add calendar from URL.
    2. Paste the link above and click Add calendar.
    3. Google checks for new bookings every few hours, so it can be slow to show new ones. The iPhone option updates faster.
    Outlook
    1. In Outlook on the web, go to Calendar → Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
    2. Paste the link above, give it a name and click Import.

    Keep this link private: anyone with it can see your bookings. If it gets shared by mistake, and subscribe again.

    2. Block times from your calendar

    Paste your own calendar's private link here. Anything in your calendar, like appointments, school runs or holidays, stops clients booking that time. Events you've set to "Free" don't block anything.

    Where do I find my calendar's link? (iPhone / iCloud)
    1. Open the Calendar app and tap Calendars at the bottom.
    2. Tap the ⓘ next to the calendar you use for personal plans.
    3. Turn on Public Calendar, tap Share Link… then Copy, and paste it above. The link is long and random, so nobody can find it unless you share it.
    Where do I find my calendar's link? (Google)
    1. On a computer, open Google Calendar, click the ⚙ Settings cog, then click your calendar's name on the left.
    2. Scroll to Integrate calendar and copy Secret address in iCal format. Paste it above.
    Where do I find my calendar's link? (Outlook)
    1. In Outlook on the web, go to Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars.
    2. Under Publish a calendar, choose your calendar and Can view all details, click Publish, then copy the ICS link and paste it above.
